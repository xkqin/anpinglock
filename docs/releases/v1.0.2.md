## 装好后，三步启用

1. **配对手机。** 在 Windows 蓝牙设置中完成配对。
2. **选择并启用。** 打开离身锁，选择手机，点击“启用离身锁屏”，等待显示已连接、保护已就绪。
3. **带上手机离开。** 手机持续断开、且电脑连续空闲满 60 秒后自动锁屏。操作键鼠会重新计时；退出界面后后台继续保护。

![Windows 蓝牙设置中已连接的手机，设备名已脱敏](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xkqin/awaylock/main/docs/assets/windows-bluetooth-connected.png)

## 这版改了什么？

- **正在使用电脑时暂缓锁屏。** 键鼠操作重新计时，蓝牙查询失败时暂停锁屏。
- **后台更轻。** 原生后台实测常驻约 14 MiB、私有内存约 2.1～2.4 MiB；退出界面后不常驻 .NET 或窗体运行时。
- **少唤醒、少写入。** 连接、会话和设置事件按需唤醒，稳定时每 30 秒更新心跳，锁屏期间暂停蓝牙服务发现。
- **保留持续保护。** 退出界面继续运行，登录/解锁和看门狗可恢复后台，同一用户只保留一个后台进程。

31 项策略检查和 8,583 次原生策略对照通过，蓝牙查询、安装更新、单实例和设置通知已做本机检查。[详细验证记录](https://github.com/xkqin/awaylock/blob/main/docs/validation.md)。

资源数字来自本机采样，打开界面会增加临时占用。蓝牙状态不能精确表示手机距离，Windows 动态锁可独立工作。本次未专门进行实际携手机离开、返回及真实锁屏的完整测试；安装包尚未做代码签名。

## English

- Prevent locking during active PC use: require both confirmed phone disconnection and 60 seconds without keyboard/mouse input. Input restarts confirmation; failed queries pause locking.
- Replace the resident managed agent with a small native C++/WinRT process. Closing the interface releases its .NET/WPF process.
- Local samples measured approximately 14 MiB resident and 2.1–2.4 MiB private memory. Two 60-second locked-idle samples used 0–15.625 ms of CPU time; the Bluetooth-query probe peaked at 14.23 MiB. The open dashboard uses additional memory, and results vary by device and state.
- Event-driven wakeups, 30-second stable heartbeats, no service discovery while locked, and fewer log writes.
- Preserve background protection after closing the UI, sign-in/unlock recovery, watchdog recovery and single-instance protection.
- Verified with 31 policy checks, 8,583 native/reference decisions, and local Bluetooth, installer, singleton and settings-event checks.

Install or upgrade using `AwayLock-Setup-1.0.2-win-x64.exe`. Windows 10 2004+ or Windows 11 x64; per-user installation. SHA-256 checksums and a device-free validation summary are attached.

Bluetooth state is not an exact distance measurement. Windows Dynamic Lock operates independently and its settings are unchanged. A deliberate physical walk-away/return and actual lock cycle was not performed. The installer is unsigned.
